# hive-kit Specification

## Purpose
A third generated derelict kit, the hive, with brood halls, shafts and resin ducts built by organic civilizations.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The hive derelict kit
A third generated derelict kit, the hive, SHALL build brood halls, tall shafts and resin ducts with brood pods on the outside, amber colours and its own detail style. Generated civilizations of organic shape SHALL build hives.

#### Scenario: Hives generate
- **WHEN** forty hive ruins are generated from different seeds
- **THEN** every one is a valid, connected ruin without falling back to the fixed layout
