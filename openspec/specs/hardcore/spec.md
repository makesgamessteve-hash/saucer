# hardcore Specification

## Purpose
Hardcore mode: a whole universe where a Fold Retreat costs everything in the hold.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Hardcore mode
Hardcore SHALL be chosen for a whole universe on the wake-up title card before anyone gets up, or with `--hardcore`, and SHALL be shown on the HUD. In Hardcore a Fold Retreat SHALL lose every hot and banked item in the hold for good (modules, the dome and its displays are kept), the Lifeline SHALL lose what was in the player's hands, and the bed SHALL offer no loading: the save made on sleeping is the only way back in, from the title card. Nothing SHALL become lethal.

#### Scenario: A Fold Retreat in Hardcore
- **WHEN** a player in Hardcore retreats with two banked finds and a module in the hold
- **THEN** the finds are gone for good and the module stays
