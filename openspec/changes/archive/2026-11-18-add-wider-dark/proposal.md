## Why

Roadmap milestone 8, "The wider dark (M2)": the one-sector game becomes a
small galaxy that two to four people can play on one screen. Fold Core jumps
connect generated sectors, generated civilizations fill them, the
Xenoarchive and trader requests give salvage a purpose beyond credits, new
non-lethal sentries and tools raise the stakes in the far sectors, and the
mystery runs on past the Resonant vault to the Heart and its endings
(concept §3.6, §5.3, §5.6, §7, §8.2-8.3).

## Impact

- New capabilities: `coop-local`, `fold-jumps`, `civilizations`,
  `xenoarchive`, `requests-reputation`, `sentries-advanced`, `mystery-heart`,
  `procedural-music`, `hive-kit`
- Builds on the mystery of `add-mystery-chain`, the sentries of
  `add-sentries-and-trade`, the co-op slots of `add-player-input-commands`
  and the on-foot rules of `add-on-foot-excursions`
- The home sector is generated exactly as before (its layout hashes, sites
  and sentries are unchanged); everything new lives in the other sectors
- Known answers: `tests/replays/slice_seed42.srep` re-recorded for state
  version 9
- No lethal weapons: Clamps, Lockdowns, decoys, the Shroud and the Override
  spike stop, fool or switch off sentries; nothing is destroyed
