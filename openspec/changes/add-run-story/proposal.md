## Why
A run has a central mystery (Who, Where, Key, the Witness, the Heart) but no story around it: the Echoes say what happened in a line each, the cities and wrecks are places and not witnesses, and two runs read the same. Steve asked for "an overarching story for each new run". The story should be told by the world: wreck logs, murals and archive records at real places, each pointing at the next, with a payoff at the Heart.

## What Changes
- Slice 1, the generator: `sim/story.lua` picks one of five plot skeletons (a civilisation vanished overnight, a war that ended both sides, a signal calling everyone home, your saucer's builders, a sleep the Watchers guard) from the universe seed, with 4 to 6 beats. It fills them from the run: the mystery's civilisation, object and place, a second civilisation, what the Echo really is, who betrayed whom, and (most runs) one twist such as "the Watchers were caretakers". Every beat is bound to a real site (home sector, a far spine sector, a body city's archive, the Heart) and ends with a lead to the next place. Text is templates plus the civs' trait words (`data/base/story/*.lua`), no runtime generator. State version 12, `world.story` in saves (progress only).
- Slice 2, clues in the world: beam candidates of kind `story` (a wreck log or a mural in a derelict hall, an archive record in a city's archive that reads only while its district's grid is on), found one after the other, shared by the whole party, filed on every player's bookshelf.
- Slice 3, the codex and the payoff: a Story page at the holotable in the living room that stitches the found clues, the Echoes and the places together; HUD hints in hints mode; the finale told when the Heart is resolved, by the chosen ending and the twist.

## Impact
Sim: new `sim/story.lua`, `data/base/story/{skeletons,twists,vocab}.lua`; `sim/game.lua` (init, pick-up, candidates), `sim/save.lua`, `sim/tractor.lua`, `sim/heart.lua`, `sim/books.lua`, `sim/home.lua`, the interior layout. View: `view/render.lua`, `view/hud.lua`. State version 12 (save format stays 3); the golden replays were re-recorded. The story is a pure function of the seed: co-op peers agree and a save keeps only progress. It is off with the mystery (Mystery: off, the endless sandbox).
