## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Acts II and III across sectors
After the Resonant vault opens (Act I), the chain SHALL continue: the Witness Echo in a site of the second spine sector (Act II) and, once it is decoded, the Heart Key in a site of the third (Act III). Their sectors SHALL be tagged RESONANT on the map; hints SHALL name the sector.

#### Scenario: Next sector
- **WHEN** the vault has just opened
- **THEN** the map tags the second spine sector RESONANT and the Witness can be found there

### Requirement: The translation gate
The Witness SHALL be decodable only when the party's best Xenoarchive percentage for its civilization is at least 25%; otherwise the lab SHALL refuse with a message naming the civilization.

#### Scenario: Not yet
- **WHEN** a player queues the Witness with 0% of its civilization archived
- **THEN** the lab refuses: "The Witness won't translate: your Xenoarchive needs 25% of the ..."

### Requirement: The Heart and the ending vote
The Heart sector SHALL hold the Heart site. Carrying the Heart Key within 45 m of the centre of its vault hall SHALL open the Heart. Every player inside the Heart site SHALL then vote on one of three endings from the map; when all of them have voted the ending SHALL be the one with most votes, a tie going to the vote of whoever set the Key. Everyone SHALL see the ending and get its book; those inside SHALL carry the centerpiece home. The vote and the ending SHALL be saved.

#### Scenario: Tie
- **WHEN** P2 sets the Key, P1 votes the first ending and P2 the second
- **THEN** the second ending wins and the mystery is over

### Requirement: Endings change the universe
Each ending SHALL apply a lasting effect: sealing makes Watchers and Snatchers less keen, restoring raises prices and request pay, taking widens Watcher sight and doubles the centerpiece's worth.

#### Scenario: Seal
- **WHEN** the party chose the seal ending
- **THEN** Watcher range is 80% of normal from then on
