## Why

The "Remaining work" run of 2026-11-18 (Saves, WebXR, Meshes, Renderer, Gameplay gaps, Co-op,
Housekeeping) left a short list of things that were deliberately not built. They are collected
here so the changes they came from could be archived. The same list is in `docs/ROADMAP.md`
under "Follow-ups".

## What Changes

- A checklist of deferred features and manual checks, grouped by the area that deferred them.
- No code: nothing here is scheduled, and each item becomes its own change when somebody picks it up.

## Impact

- None on the game. No spec deltas (`skip_specs`).
