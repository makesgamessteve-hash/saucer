## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Light switches
Keys 4, 5 and 6 SHALL toggle the headlights, the floods and the cabin lamps. One gamepad button (Share, the touchpad or a back paddle) SHALL toggle the headlights on a tap (the cabin lamps while walking the Villa) and the floods on a half-second hold. Each switch SHALL show a message.

#### Scenario: Pad hold
- **WHEN** a player holds the pad's light button for half a second at the helm
- **THEN** the floods switch and the headlights stay as they were
