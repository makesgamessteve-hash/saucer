## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Tow bill
A Fold Retreat in a danger-2 or danger-3 sector SHALL cost a tow bill (60 c and 120 c by default). It SHALL be paid from credits first; what the player cannot pay SHALL come out of the Charge, never below zero, and nothing else SHALL be taken. At danger 1 a Fold Retreat SHALL stay free. The player SHALL be told what was paid.

#### Scenario: Broke at danger 2
- **WHEN** a player with 20 c and 100 Charge folds back in a danger-2 sector
- **THEN** they pay 20 c and 40 Charge and keep their hold, Echoes and banked cargo
