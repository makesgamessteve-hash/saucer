## Targets missed (known gaps)

- **BeamOnly still out-earns careful play.** Risk now costs (5.4 retreats, 131 c of tow bills, half the hot
  cargo), but the Human-Proxy career bot pays more: it dives guarded sites in the Seat, gets shot once seen
  up close, heads home at hull < 60 with fewer items and banks once per 20 minutes (529 c vs 996 c before).
  The bot does not sneak, jam or decoy on its own, so this mostly measures the bot; a stealth-aware bot
  policy (or softer Seat damage) is the next step.
- **First threat for the human bot is 13.7 min, not <= 3 min.** The light Watcher at site 1 meets the Villa
  within a minute (BeamOnly 0.8 min, screenshot 10), but the career bot visits site 1 in the Seat, which the
  light Watcher only sees within 15 m.
- **Near-misses 0.23 / site-minute**, below the 2-6 band.
