# Playtest, 2026-11-17: ten long bot sessions

Ten sessions of 30–40 minutes of in-game time each, on v0.2.0 (main at 4c93571) and then on
branch `playtest-1` with the fixes below. Each session was played by the scripted bot in
`tools/playtest/` and measured by `tools/playtest/analyze.py`. The numbers are from the final
batch, which used the fixed build and the final harness (`playtest_out/final/`).

**What a bot can and can't tell you.** The bot measures time, money, deaths, stalls, waits and
softlocks, and it finds what blocks progress. It **cannot** judge feel: flight handling, camera
comfort, readability at a glance, the audio, how the art lands, or whether the mystery text is
intriguing. It also plays better than a new player in some places (it knows where everything
is) and worse in others (it lines up the tractor beam slowly and never cuts a corner). Read the
"fun" section as a designer reading telemetry, not as a substitute for people playing.

## How to run it

```
tools/playtest/run.sh run01 mystery 42 108000 out/run01              # headless telemetry (~80 s)
tools/playtest/run.sh run09 coop 42 108000 out/run09 headless -- --local-players 2 --viewports 2
tools/playtest/run.sh run10 net 42 108000 out/run10 net -- --net-delay 3   # two processes on loopback
SHOT_TICKS='[19140]="tractor"' STOP_TICKS=20000 tools/playtest/run.sh run05 hardcore 2026 108000 out/s05 shots -- --hardcore
python3 tools/playtest/analyze.py out/run01/run01.log [--json]
```

The `shots` mode replays the same deterministic session in a window (Xvfb) to take pictures.
Under software GL (llvmpipe) that runs at ~105 ticks/s, about 9.5 ms a frame at 1280x720,
against ~0.7–1.1 ms a tick for the simulation alone (1.6 ms on station visits). On a real GPU the
render cost will be far lower; this box can't measure it.

## Playability: the top issues

1. **The hull never mended (fixed).** `hull.regen` only recharged shields. The Steady buff
   (Sunfruit omelette, Hull-plate stout) promises "the hull mends twice as fast" and did
   nothing. After a Fold Retreat (hull 30) a player with no credits had no way back to health
   except selling. Runs 03 and 04 sat at hull 30–34 for 20+ minutes because of it.
2. **A fresh player can fold to danger 2 at once.** Run 03 jumped at 2:45 with 0 credits and
   spent the next 12 minutes failing dives. Nothing warns that the Villa can't survive a
   guarded site.
3. **The Villa can't do guarded sites; you have to use the Seat.** Every Villa dive into a
   guarded site ended in Watcher locks and usually a retreat (run 04: 13 retreats). The game
   never says "use the Seat here". The HUD shows danger, but not what it means for your size.
4. **Repeated Fold Retreats cost only time.** That's deliberate (non-lethal), but run 04 shows
   the loop is joyless: retreat, fly back, retreat. The cache is safe, so there's no tension
   either.
5. **Pickpocket every visit.** In run 07 the thief struck on 7 of 7 visits. It's fun once.
   By the third time it's a chore.
6. **Key overloading.** X is Shroud at the helm and the grapple on foot. Even the code comment
   had it wrong. The bot's first grapple attempts failed for exactly this reason.
7. **HUD text runs off the screen at 1280x720.** The control hint line and long messages are
   cut off at the right edge (see `run01_title.png`). And the helm control hint
   ("WASD fly … SPACE/CTRL up/down") is shown while you are walking the dome.
8. **Alerts pile up.** In a danger-3 derelict, four messages land at once ("A Clamp drone…",
   "THE WARDEN has seen you…", "LOCKDOWN…", "CLAMPED…"). Each is cut off at the screen
   edge, and the most important one ("hold Boost") is at the bottom (`run08_warden_seen.png`).
   Two smaller things: the station HUD overprints "SAUCER: 3 IN YOUR POCKET" with the pocket
   count, and the lab shows "ID 10% (PAUSED)" inside derelicts, which adds to the lab
   bottleneck below.
9. **The scrap dealer buys anything, for very little.** Run 07 sold a "Data core
   (undecoded)" for 10 c without being warned (`run07_station_market.png`).
10. **TV distances read "0 km"** for nearby stations (fixed: now metres under 1 km).

## Pacing

- **Time to first loot: 1:30–5:10** (7:16 for the greedy run abroad). Good. The tutorial site at
  ~150 m does its job.
- **The first ten minutes are strong; minutes 10–25 sag.** Credits at 10, 20 and 30 minutes were
  typically 0–375, 0–506 and 1–795. The only big sinks are Fold Core tiers (450/900 c) and
  furniture. Most runs bought T2 the moment they could and then had nothing to save for.
- **The lab is the pacing bottleneck.** Identify takes 30 s × tier per item and the queue is
  FIFO, so an Echo decode (20 s) waits behind a hold full of Identify jobs. That accounts for
  most dead air: run 01 had 5 gaps over 2 minutes (16 min total), and run 02, which waits for
  identification before selling, made its first sale at 20:32.
- **Walking the saucer takes 15–30% of play time** (50% in the home-life run, 55% for the
  co-op P2). Each Villa-room grab is a walk down to the Tractor Room and back: about
  **15 s a find** (930 ticks). Runs that mostly grab from the Seat average ~8 s. That's charming the first dozen
  times. On a 12-slot hold it becomes the job.
- **Travel versus activity.** Open-space travel was 6–24% of time; inside sites 35–60%.
  Healthy. Sites are close enough.
- **Home sector runs dry at about 15–20 minutes for two players.** Act I took 23–33 minutes. In
  co-op the looter (P2) spent most of the second half waiting for the mystery player to be
  ready to fold.
- **Home life has the best rhythm.** Run 06 had no gap over 2 minutes and earned the most,
  because the bot cooked, read and played the holodeck while the lab worked. The home loop
  fills the dead air. That's the strongest pacing argument for it.

## Fun (a designer's reading of the telemetry)

- **The core loop works:** line up over a find, beam it up, watch it shrink, bank it on the way
  out. First loot within 5 minutes and Act I within ~25–30 minutes is a good arc for a first
  session.
- **Risk is binary.** In the Seat at danger 1 nothing threatens you. In the Villa at danger 2+ you
  lose. Retreats are free apart from time, so neither case is tense. Hardcore (run 05) lost
  nothing because the bot never retreated with hot cargo. Hardcore only bites if you push your
  luck, and nothing in the game tempts you to.
- **Excursions are the most varied content.** Crawlspace, Delicate find, hand-lock, grid
  console, grapple, cutter and the Warden all worked end to end at danger 3. But the Warden
  ended all three danger-3 excursions in its "disabled" state, once the bot had worked the
  grid console. It's frightening for about one room, then it's gone.
- **Co-op works but doesn't need two players.** Nothing asks for two saucers. Two players
  claim different sites and play solo side by side, then one waits for the other at the
  fold.
- **Money has nothing to buy after T2.** That's the biggest hole for minutes 15–40.

## Bugs fixed (branch `playtest-1`)

- `e162a23` **The hull now mends slowly in open space** (0.1 points/s after the 4 s no-hit delay;
  never inside a site and never from 0), so **Steady now doubles it** as its text says. After a
  Fold Retreat a new message points at slow mending and the trade terminal. **TV rows** show
  metres under 1 km. The grapple comment in `sim/tools.lua` is fixed. OpenSpec change
  `fix-playtest-1`; unit tests in `tests/lua/loop_spec.lua` and `tests/lua/homelife_spec.lua`.
- `aac55c5` `--ticks-per-frame N` for windowed scripts. This lets long screenshot replays draw one
  frame every N ticks, and it still captures a requested screenshot on its own tick. The C unit
  test is in `tests/c/test_engine.c`.
- The harness itself: `tools/playtest/` (bot, ten styles, runner, analyzer, `SHOT_TICKS`/`STOP_TICKS`/`TPF`).

Checks: 37/37 ctest, coverage C 98.2% and Lua 98.15% (gate 95%), `openspec validate --all
--strict` 35/35, replays unchanged.

## Recommendations (prioritised; design changes, not made here)

1. **Something to spend on after T2:** saucer and module upgrades at traders (tractor speed, a
   bigger hold, shield capacity, a lab slot). That fills minutes 15–40.
2. **A second lab slot, or Echo decodes jumping the queue.** That removes most of the dead air.
3. **Warn before folding into danger the saucer can't handle:** "Danger 2: Watchers will lock a
   Villa. Dive in the Seat." It could also be a soft gate (needs T2, or a first Seat dive).
4. **Make the Seat the obvious choice in guarded sites,** with a hint the first time a Watcher
   locks on to a Villa.
5. **Cut the walking per find:** let the Tractor Room keep a short queue of lined-up targets, or
   give the helm a remote beam for small items after an upgrade.
6. **Pickpocket:** a chance per visit (say 25%) with a cooldown, not every visit.
7. **Give retreats a cost** at danger 2+ (a repair bill, or Charge), so pushing on is a decision.
8. **Keep the Warden awake** after the grid console: slower, but still patrolling.
9. **Co-op reasons to be together:** two-saucer finds (one holds a shutter while the other
   beams), shared requests, a "ready to fold" indicator for the waiting player.
10. **Bind the grapple to its own key** and wrap or shorten HUD text at 1280x720. Show only
    the hints for the current mode, and put the most urgent alert first.
11. **Warn before selling story items** (data cores, Echoes) at a stall.
