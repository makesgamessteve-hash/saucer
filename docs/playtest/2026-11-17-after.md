# Playtest, 2026-11-17 (after): the eleven recommendations

The same ten bot sessions as in [2026-11-17.md](2026-11-17.md) (same seeds, styles, lengths
and harness), rerun on branch `playtest-recs` with the eleven recommendations of that report
implemented (OpenSpec change `add-playtest-recs`). "Before" is `playtest_out/final/`, "after"
is `playtest_out/after/`. Both batches were read by the same `tools/playtest/analyze.py`, which
now also counts upgrades, tow bills, fold warnings, Seat tips, relay pulls, pickpocket strikes,
twin vaults and the co-op follower's waiting time (so the "before" co-op idle numbers below are
recomputed, not copied from the first report).

The bot was taught the new things a player would do: it buys upgrades when it can keep a
150 c reserve, reads the fold warning and folds anyway, keeps story items when the sale warning
appears, delivers party requests when it holds a match, says "I'm ready to fold" when it has
nothing left to loot, and in co-op the follower holds a twin vault's anchor while the leader
beams the vault. The bot still plays its fixed style, so a number that does not move often
means the bot never reached the new thing, not that the change does nothing. See "Reading the
numbers".

No crashes, no Lua errors and no desyncs in any run. Run 10 (network co-op) again ended with
identical hashes on both machines (2,094 hash checks, final hash de48a8d8501e4bbf at tick 125,645).

## Reading the numbers

- **Runs 01, 02, 03: no change.** Same play to the tick. These bots reach Fold Core T2 with
  under 10 c left and never have 330 c (an upgrade plus the reserve) before the end, and they
  never queue a decode behind a long Identify. The dead air in runs 01 and 02 (14–16 min) is
  still the bot waiting for its single Identify queue; upgrades don't fix that unless a
  player earns the 350 c for the second bench. Run 03 now sees the fold warning (5 lines,
  including "You have 0 c") and the Seat tip, and pays one 60 c tow bill; the bot folds
  anyway, as its style dictates.
- **Run 04 (sandbox, 13 retreats):** walking 29.6% → 24.8% with the relay (3 relay pulls),
  dead air 29.5 → 27.1 min, but the same 13 retreats: the bot ignores the fold warning and the
  Seat tip by design. It paid 8 tow bills (540 c), which is exactly the cost the report asked
  for: credits at 10 min 374 → 279 because the relay was bought, then broke from 20 min on.
- **Run 05 (hardcore):** walking 30.4% → 27.1% (relay). Credits at 20 min 207 → 27 because of
  the relay purchase.
- **Run 06 (home life):** same rhythm (0 dead air); bought the relay (credits 795 → 615).
- **Run 07 (stations):** pickpocket strikes **7 → 2** in the same seven visits. Dead air
  10.8 → 12.9 min: the thief was a regular "meaningful event" before. The bot now heeds the
  sale warning and kept the data core at the stall.
- **Run 08 (Warden excursions):** walking 7.3% → 3.7%, retreats 2 → 1, one 120 c tow bill.
  Dead air went up (24.1 → 27.8 min) because the bot spent the last ten minutes stuck
  travelling at danger 3 (a bot pathing issue, "bots still running 10 min past the limit").
- **Run 09 (split-screen co-op):** the follower's partner wait **10.0 → 0.8 min** and walking
  54.8% → 20.2%. But it did not pay off: P2 beamed a twin anchor and hovered by it for about
  ten minutes, P1 never came (it was mid-dive and then trading), so P2 ended with nothing sold
  (earned 224 → 0). P1 bought the relay and tractor coils (earned 1054 → 980).
- **Run 10 (network co-op):** **the twin vault was opened** (P2 held the anchor, P1 beamed
  it, a prize each) and P1 solved Act I again. Idle at the helm 3.9 → 0.2 min (P1) and
  partner wait 4.0 → 0.0 min (P2); walking P2 21.9% → 10.1%. Dead air rose because the hold
  took 6–8 minutes of waiting by the anchor.

**Verdict.** The recommendations that change the *rules* show up clearly: the pickpocket
strikes 2 times instead of 7, Fold Retreats now cost money (60–540 c a run), the relay cuts
walking by 3–5 points where it was bought, and the warning and tip fire where they should.
The ones that depend on *money* (upgrades, the second bench) hardly show, because the bot's
economy still runs at 200–600 c per half hour and it spends on Fold Core first. In real play
the upgrades are the late-game sink the report asked for; whether they are priced right needs
people. Co-op idle is down but the twin vault asks for coordination the follower bot can't
always get; a human pair will talk to each other.

## Gaps

- No queue of beam targets in the Tractor Room; walking is cut only by the relay (S and M).
- The twin anchor and the twin vault sit on loot slots: a beam over them takes the find on
  that slot first, then the anchor or vault on the next pull.
- The first-seen state of the HUD hints lives in the view, not the save.
- Upgrade prices are set by reasoning, not by the bot's economy (the bot rarely affords them).
- A tour script that set `p.hints` from outside froze the windowed render (the sim kept
  going); not reproduced in play, not investigated further.
- The bot follower in split-screen co-op can wait by a twin anchor for its partner in vain.
